Principles of open source bioinstrumentation applied to the poseidon syringe pump system

Versions
Author Correction: Principles of open source bioinstrumentation applied to the poseidon syringe pump system Correction · 2023-09-08 Principles of open source bioinstrumentation applied to the poseidon syringe pump system Scientific Reports · 2019-08-27 Design principles for open source bioinstrumentation: the poseidon syringe pump system as an example bioRxiv · 2019-05-03

Summary

Instrumentation to perform single-cell RNA-sequencing experiments is costly and limits the scale of experimentation. To address this, we have developed an open-source syringe pump and microscope system called poseidon. The system of three syringe pumps and microscope costs less than $400, and can be assembled in under an hour. Importantly, poseidon reduces costs of experimentation by up to 25x while demonstrating similar accuracy and precision to commercial alternatives.
