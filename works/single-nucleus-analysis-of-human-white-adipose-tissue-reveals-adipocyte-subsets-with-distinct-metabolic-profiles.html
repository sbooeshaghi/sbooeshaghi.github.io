Single-Nucleus Analysis of Human White Adipose Tissue Reveals Adipocyte Subsets with Distinct Metabolic Profiles

Human white adipose tissue contributes to metabolic disease, but depot-level measurements can miss functionally distinct adipocyte states. The challenge is resolving adipocyte heterogeneity and linking those states to obesity, glycemia, diabetes, and regulators of adipogenesis. Single-nucleus profiling identifies ADIPOMAThi and ADIPOMATlo adipocytes with distinct metabolic profiles and implicates TSHZ3 as an in vivo regulator of adipose tissue development.
