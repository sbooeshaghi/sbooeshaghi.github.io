kallisto, bustools and kb-python for quantifying bulk, single-cell and single-nucleus RNA-seq

RNA-seq preprocessing is central to turning sequencing reads into expression measurements for bulk tissues, single cells, and single nuclei. The challenge is that mapping, barcode correction, UMI handling, reference construction, and assay-specific settings require multiple coordinated steps. The protocol gives a reproducible kallisto, bustools, and kb-python workflow that reduces preprocessing to reference generation and quantification while supporting standard, nascent-and-cDNA, bulk, single-cell, and single-nucleus analyses.
