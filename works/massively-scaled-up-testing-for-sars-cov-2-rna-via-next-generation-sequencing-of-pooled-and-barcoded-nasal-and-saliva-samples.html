Massively scaled-up testing for SARS-CoV-2 RNA via next-generation sequencing of pooled and barcoded nasal and saliva samples

Frequent asymptomatic testing can suppress viral transmission, but RT-qPCR capacity, extraction workflows, and cost limited population-scale SARS-CoV-2 screening. SwabSeq addresses that bottleneck by combining sample barcodes, end-point RT-PCR, and synthetic RNA standards so pooled sequencing can call thousands of nasal or saliva specimens without RNA extraction. Clinical validation and deployment showed qPCR-comparable performance, extraction-free workflows, and more than 80,000 returned tests with sub-24-hour turnaround.
