Reliable and accurate diagnostics from highly multiplexed sequencing assays

Scalable, inexpensive, and secure SARS-CoV-2 testing is essential for pandemic control, and highly multiplexed sequencing assays promise greater throughput and lower cost than RT-qPCR. Their clinical use depends on reliable computational processing, interpretation, and classification. A kallisto-bustools workflow processes proposed SARS-CoV-2 sequencing assays quickly and accurately with low memory use, and generalizes to diagnostic assays with different targets and barcode structures.
