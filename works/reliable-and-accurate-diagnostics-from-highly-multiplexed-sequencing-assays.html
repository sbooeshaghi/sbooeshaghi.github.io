Reliable and accurate diagnostics from highly multiplexed sequencing assays

Scalable infectious-disease testing depends on software that can turn multiplexed sequencing reads into reliable clinical calls. Sequencing-based SARS-CoV-2 assays promise high throughput, but they require transparent, portable, statistically sound analysis pipelines. The kallisto-bustools HMSA workflow processes SwabSeq and related assays quickly, validates sample classification and viral-load estimation, and generalizes to multiple diagnostic sequencing designs.
