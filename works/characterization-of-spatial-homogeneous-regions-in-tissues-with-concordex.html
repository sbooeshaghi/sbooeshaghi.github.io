Characterization of spatial homogeneous regions in tissues with concordex

Spatial transcriptomics can reveal tissue organization, but spatially variable gene calls are hard to interpret when cell-type composition and location are entangled. Existing domain methods may identify regions without separating cell-type effects from spatial effects or their interactions. concordex defines spatial homogeneous regions from kNN neighborhood composition, works with discrete or continuous labels across SRT platforms, and pairs those regions with a negative-binomial GLM to identify subtle spatial and cell-type interaction patterns.
