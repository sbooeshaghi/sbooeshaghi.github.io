Characterization of spatial homogeneous regions in tissues with concordex

Spatially resolved transcriptomics can reveal gene-expression variation across tissues, but cell-type distributions confound the detection of spatially variable genes. Analyses therefore need to separate cell-type effects from cell-type-independent spatial effects. concordex identifies cell-type-homogeneous domains efficiently across platforms and tissues, enabling this decomposition and exposing subtle variation missed by cell-type-only or spatial-only analyses.
