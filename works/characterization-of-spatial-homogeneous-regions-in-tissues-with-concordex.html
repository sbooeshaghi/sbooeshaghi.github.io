Characterization of spatial homogeneous regions in tissues with concordex

Summary

Spatial omics data can contain regions of tissue with distinct molecular organization. This work extends the concordex framework to characterize spatially homogeneous regions in tissues by measuring neighborhood concordance in spatial genomics data.
