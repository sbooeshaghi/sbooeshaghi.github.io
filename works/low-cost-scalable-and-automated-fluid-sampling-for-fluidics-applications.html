Low-cost, scalable, and automated fluid sampling for fluidics applications

Automated fraction collection is useful for microfluidics and chromatography, but commercial systems can be costly, bulky, and difficult to customize. Colosseum addresses this access gap with an open-source, 3D-printed, single-motor fraction collector that can be assembled cheaply from off-the-shelf parts. Bench tests and gradient collections show accurate, scalable sampling across common fluidics applications.
