Low-cost, scalable, and automated fluid sampling for fluidics applications

Versions
Low-cost, scalable, and automated fluid sampling for fluidics applications HardwareX · 2021-05-31

Summary

Current commercial systems for long-time-course microfluidics experiments are costly and close-source, limiting the scale of experimentation and hardware customization. To address this, we have developed a low-cost, modular, and automated fluid sampling device for scalable fluidic applications called colosseum. The colosseum fraction collector uses a single motor, and can be built for less than $100, demonstrating a cost savings of ~10x compared to commercial alternatives. Colosseum uses off-the- shelf and 3D-printed components and can be assembled in less than an hour making it accessible to researchers in low-resource environments.
