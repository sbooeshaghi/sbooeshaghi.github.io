Low-cost, scalable, and automated fluid sampling for fluidics applications

Automated fraction collection is useful for microfluidics and chromatography, but commercial systems can be costly, bulky, and difficult to customize. Colosseum addresses this access gap with an open-source, 3D-printed, single-motor fraction collector that can be assembled cheaply from off-the-shelf parts. The open-source, single-motor Colosseum fraction collector provides accurate and scalable automated sampling using inexpensive 3D-printed and off-the-shelf components.
