Pseudoalignment facilitates assignment of error-prone Ultima Genomics reads

Lower-cost sequencing could expand single-cell genomics and clinical sequencing, but new platforms must preserve gene-level accuracy as well as throughput. Ultima Genomics reads in this PBMC comparison showed elevated homopolymer-associated errors that reduced standard alignment for genes such as TMSB4X. Pseudoalignment in kallisto-bustools recovered substantially more error-prone reads than alignment, while still leaving residual bias when error rates were very high.
