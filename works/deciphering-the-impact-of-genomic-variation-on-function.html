Deciphering the impact of genomic variation on function

Human genomic variation is a route to understanding biology, disease risk, diagnosis, and therapy. The central difficulty is that variant effects depend on molecular function, cell type, context, population, and networks, making comprehensive experimental mapping impossible by brute force. The IGVF Consortium establishes a coordinated map-perturb-predict program that will combine single-cell maps, large-scale perturbations, predictive models, benchmarks, and an open catalog of variant effects.
