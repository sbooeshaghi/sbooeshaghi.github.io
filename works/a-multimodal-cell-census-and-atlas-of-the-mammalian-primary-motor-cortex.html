A multimodal cell census and atlas of the mammalian primary motor cortex

Understanding motor cortex organization requires a reference that connects molecular cell identities to anatomy, physiology, projections, spatial location, and species conservation. Before BICCN integration, these modalities were often measured in separate studies, leaving correspondence among cell-type definitions uncertain. The atlas combines coordinated mouse, marmoset, and human datasets into a conserved hierarchical taxonomy and registers multimodal phenotypes, wiring, genetic tools, and gene-regulatory features into a shared primary motor cortex framework.
