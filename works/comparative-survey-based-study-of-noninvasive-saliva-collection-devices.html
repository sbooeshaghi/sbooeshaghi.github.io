Comparative survey-based study of noninvasive saliva collection devices

Saliva diagnostics matter because whole saliva can be collected noninvasively and can support applications ranging from infectious disease testing to biomarker monitoring. Yet collection protocols are not standardized, and device usability, leakage, and collection efficiency can affect sample quality and user safety. By comparing five representative devices in 60 adults, the work identifies tradeoffs across metrics and motivates better standardized, user-friendly saliva collection designs.
