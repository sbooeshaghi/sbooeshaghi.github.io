Comparative survey-based study of noninvasive saliva collection devices

Saliva is a valuable noninvasive diagnostic sample, but collection-device usability can affect its diagnostic effectiveness. Five swab, funnel, and passive-drool designs were evaluated with 60 healthy adults using collection measurements and user surveys. Device performance varied substantially, and no evaluated device combined efficient, easy, safe, and leak-free collection across all requirements.
