Voyager: exploratory single-cell genomics data analysis with geospatial statistics

Spatial organization is central to tissue function, and spatial omics needs exploratory tools that can reason directly over geometry, neighborhoods, and autocorrelation. Existing single-cell frameworks only partially incorporate the geospatial ESDA tradition and can also produce language-dependent results across R and Python implementations. Voyager combines SpatialFeatureExperiment geometry support, scalable univariate/bivariate/multivariate ESDA, reproducible tutorials, and compatibility-tested R/Python implementations to reveal signals such as library-size biology and negative spatial autocorrelation.
