Determining gene specificity from multivariate single-cell RNA sequencing data

Single-cell atlases can reveal where genes are specific, but marker interpretation becomes complicated when variation spans cell types, tissues, strains, and individuals. Existing specificity measures do not always satisfy clear information-theoretic properties or handle multivariate context. ember introduces entropy-based metrics for gene specificity and applies them to mouse and human datasets, revealing context-dependent markers, housekeeping genes, strain effects, and PBMC variation localized to classical monocytes.
