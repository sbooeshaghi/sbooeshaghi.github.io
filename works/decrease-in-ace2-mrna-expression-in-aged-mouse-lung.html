Decrease in ACE2 mRNA expression in aged mouse lung

bioRxiv 2020-04-05

Summary

COVID-19 mortality has been reported higher in older individuals and one hypothesis is age-related variation in ACE2, a receptor for SARS-CoV-2 viral entry. To study this question of age-related changes in ACE2, we analyzed single-cell RNAseq data in mouse lung and showed that 24-month old mice had significantly reduced ACE2 mRNA expression relative to 3-month old mice. We find that these differences appear to be localized to ciliated cells.
