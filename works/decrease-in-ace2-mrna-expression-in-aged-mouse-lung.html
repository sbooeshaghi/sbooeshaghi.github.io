Decrease in ACE2 mRNA expression in aged mouse lung

Understanding why COVID-19 severity rises with age requires evidence about host factors such as ACE2 in relevant tissues. Available human data were limited, and speculation about age-dependent ACE2 expression needed cell-type-resolved analysis. Analysis of young and old mouse lung single-cell and bulk RNA-seq finds reduced ACE2 mRNA with age, localized partly to ciliated-cell expression and abundance changes, while TMPRSS2 did not show the same pattern.
