Decrease in ACE2 mRNA expression in aged mouse lung

Understanding why COVID-19 severity rises with age requires evidence about host factors such as ACE2 in relevant tissues. Available human data were limited, and speculation about age-dependent ACE2 expression needed cell-type-resolved analysis. Young-versus-old mouse lung analyses show reduced ACE2 mRNA with age, partly reflecting ciliated-cell expression and abundance changes, without a corresponding TMPRSS2 pattern.
