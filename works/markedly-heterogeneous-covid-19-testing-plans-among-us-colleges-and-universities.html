Markedly heterogeneous COVID-19 testing plans among US colleges and universities

Safe campus reopening during COVID-19 depended on testing plans that could identify infections before outbreaks spread. U.S. colleges faced conflicting guidance, uneven resources, and opaque public communication, producing wide variation in whether and how communities would be screened. A survey of more than 500 institutions documents heterogeneous testing strategies, divergence from emerging best practices for frequent testing, and equity concerns tied to institutional resources and worker access.
