Markedly heterogeneous COVID-19 testing plans among US colleges and universities

US colleges preparing to reopen in fall 2020 needed mitigation plans capable of limiting SARS-CoV-2 spread and rapidly detecting outbreaks. Public communications about those plans were often opaque. A review of testing plans from more than 500 institutions found highly variable and muddled strategies, ranging from regular community testing to no testing at all.
