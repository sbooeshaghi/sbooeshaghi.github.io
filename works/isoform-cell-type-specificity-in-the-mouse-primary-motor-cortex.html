Isoform cell-type specificity in the mouse primary motor cortex

Isoform regulation is central to neuronal differentiation and disease, but most single-cell brain atlases collapse transcripts to gene-level counts. That gene-level view can miss cell-type-specific isoform switches and spatial patterns that matter for interpreting neuronal diversity. By linking SMART-seq isoform quantification with 10x cell-type assignments and MERFISH spatial data, the study builds a mouse MOp isoform atlas and identifies isoform markers, shifts, and regulatory programs hidden from gene-level analysis.
