A machine-readable specification for genomics assays

Accurate preprocessing of genomics reads depends on knowing how biological and technical sequences are arranged in each library. The gap is that assay-specific scripts, divergent parameters, and non-machine-readable library descriptions make uniform processing, comparison, and reanalysis difficult. Seqspec contributes a YAML-based specification and command-line tool for annotating library regions, validating specifications, and generating tool-compatible preprocessing inputs for standardized genomics workflows.
