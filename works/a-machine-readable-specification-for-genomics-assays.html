A machine-readable specification for genomics assays

Bioinformatics 2024-03-29 bioRxiv 2023-03-21

Summary

Sequencing-based genomics assays produce sequencing reads with a variety of synthetic and biological features. Often base-pair-level annotations of these sequencing reads are hard to find, incomplete, or are altogether missing from the associated data inhibiting uniform preprocessing. To overcome this, we developed seqspec, a machine readable specification and associated command-line tool for annotating sequencing reads. seqspec is general and applicable to all genomics-based assays and facilitates standardization of preprocessing, tracking, and comparison of genomics assays.
