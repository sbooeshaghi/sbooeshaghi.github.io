Thermo-electrochemical generator: energy harvesting & thermoregulation for liquid cooling applications

Recovering low-grade waste heat matters because data centers and other liquid-cooled systems spend substantial energy removing heat that is otherwise unused. Conventional thermoelectric approaches face material, geometry, and transport limitations, especially at low temperatures. The flow thermo-electrochemical cell couples liquid cooling with redox-based energy harvesting, demonstrating convective cooling, measurable power output, and a path toward temperature monitoring and recycled power in liquid-cooled infrastructure.
