Assessing the multimodal tradeoff

bioRxiv 2023-04-18

Summary

Multi-modal single-cell genomics assays quantify genomic features, such as gene expression and chromatin accessibility in the same cell. While single-cell registration can help better understand the coordination of distinct biological processes, an assessment of the multimodal and unimodal tradeoff has not yet been undertaken. To understand this tradeoff, we develop a snATAK, a preprocessing workflow for chromatin accessibility and “multiome” assays. We show concordance with state-of-the-art, and demonstrate improved speed and runtime. Using snATAK we find that multimodal ATAC/RNA yields fewer peaks detected per cell, lower library complexity, and increased technical noise when compared to their unimodal counterparts. Additionally, we show that registered multimodal data can be used to find allele-concordance between the ATAC and RNA parts of 10x Multiome and SHARE-seqV2 data.
