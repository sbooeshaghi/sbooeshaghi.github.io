Algorithms for a Commons Cell Atlas

Cell atlases organize representative datasets, cell types, and marker genes across tissues. Manual duplicated curation, inconsistent preprocessing, and a lack of compatible tools make large atlases difficult to reprocess and update. The Commons Cell Atlas supplies validated infrastructure for fast iterative processing, including modular count-matrix workflows that support human and other organism atlases.
