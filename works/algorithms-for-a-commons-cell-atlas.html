Algorithms for a Commons Cell Atlas

Cell atlases are valuable because they organize single-cell datasets into reference maps of tissues, cell types, and marker genes. Current atlas projects remain difficult to update or jointly analyze because preprocessing is inconsistent, marker curation is manual, and compatible reanalysis tools are limited. The work introduces mx, ec, cellatlas, and Commons Cell Atlas infrastructure for automated filtering, normalization, cell-type assignment, marker updating, and reproducible atlas construction.
