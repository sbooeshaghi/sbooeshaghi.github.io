Systematic cell-type resolved transcriptomes of 8 tissues in 8 lab and wild-derived mouse strains capture global and local expression variation

Genetic diversity shapes physiology through cell-type-specific programs, so resolving expression variation at cellular resolution is essential for connecting genotype to disease. Bulk tissue profiles and limited strain panels can obscure where strain and sex effects concentrate. The Cell Genomics resource maps eight Collaborative Cross founder strains across eight tissue groups, showing that genotype-driven and disease-relevant signatures localize to particular cell types.
