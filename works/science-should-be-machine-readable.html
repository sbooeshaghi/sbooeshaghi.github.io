Science should be machine-readable

Scientific papers contain results that machines could help audit and compare, but prose-first publication leaves those claims difficult to recover reliably after the fact. The gap is a lack of structured claims, evidence, and evaluations produced at publication scale. OpenEval demonstrates a machine-automated pipeline over the full eLife corpus and argues that machine-readable science should be generated during publication rather than reconstructed later.
