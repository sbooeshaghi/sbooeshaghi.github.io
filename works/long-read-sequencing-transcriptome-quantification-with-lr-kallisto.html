Long-read sequencing transcriptome quantification with lr-kallisto

Versions
Long-read sequencing transcriptome quantification with lr-kallisto PLOS Computational Biology · 2025-12-01 Long-read sequencing transcriptome quantification with lr-kallisto bioRxiv · 2024-07-19

Summary

Long-read RNA sequencing can resolve transcript isoforms but requires efficient quantification methods. This work introduces lr-kallisto for transcriptome quantification from long-read sequencing data.
