Quantifying orthogonal barcodes for sequence census assays

Multimodal and perturbation assays increasingly depend on short orthogonal barcodes to connect sequencing reads with proteins, samples, guides, and other features. Assay-specific tools and barcode design failures can introduce ambiguous assignment, runtime costs, and inconsistent processing across technologies. kITE uses kallisto/bustools mismatch-index pseudoalignment to quantify diverse barcode assays efficiently, and qcbc adds design diagnostics that expose shared subsequences, homopolymers, distance issues, and other error modes.
