The impact of package selection and versioning on single-cell RNA-seq analysis

Most single-cell RNA-seq analyses use Seurat or Scanpy across a multistep workflow. Package implementations and versions can silently change results and downstream interpretation. Seurat and Scanpy differed across multiple workflow stages by amounts comparable to using 5% of reads or 20% of cells, while version changes particularly altered differential-expression results.
