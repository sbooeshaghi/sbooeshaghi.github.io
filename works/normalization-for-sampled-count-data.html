Normalization for sampled count data

Reliable genomics count analysis depends on normalization that preserves biological structure while removing technical effects. Common single-cell workflows still leave residual depth structure, alter within-cell ranks, or impose dense-memory costs. The 2026 v4 formulation defines PFlog as an Anscombe-calibrated shifted centered-log-ratio transform, proves it uniquely satisfies the desired equivariant criteria, and benchmarks it across hundreds of datasets with improved depth, rank, and geometry behavior.
