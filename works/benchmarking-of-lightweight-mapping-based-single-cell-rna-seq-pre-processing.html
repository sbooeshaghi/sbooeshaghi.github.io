Benchmarking of lightweight-mapping based single-cell RNA-seq pre-processing

Growing single-cell RNA-seq datasets require preprocessing tools that are both accurate and computationally efficient. Technology-specific solutions do not scale well, creating practical cost and memory constraints. kallisto-bustools and Salmon-Alevin-fry produced similar downstream results, but kallisto-bustools was substantially faster, used less memory, and remained compatible with free cloud infrastructure.
