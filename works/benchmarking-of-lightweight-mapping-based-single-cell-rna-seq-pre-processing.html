Benchmarking of lightweight-mapping based single-cell RNA-seq pre-processing

Single-cell RNA-seq preprocessing is a prerequisite for interpreting growing cellular datasets, so users need tools that are both reproducible and computationally practical. The challenge is deciding whether competing lightweight-mapping workflows differ meaningfully in output, usability, speed, or memory. A comparison across 20 datasets finds kallisto-bustools and Salmon-Alevin-fry produce nearly identical downstream results, while Salmon-Alevin-fry is slower, more memory-intensive, and more expensive to run.
